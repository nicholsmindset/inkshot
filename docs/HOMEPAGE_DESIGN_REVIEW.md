# Homepage design review — 21 November 2026

## Reference review

Reviewed the public pages of [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/), [AdCreative.ai](https://www.adcreative.ai/) and [Pencil](https://trypencil.com/). This is a qualitative review of positioning, page content and product presentation, not a measured conversion benchmark or a claim that their conversion results transfer to Inkshot.

| Reference | Relevant pattern | Inkshot adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creatify | Website input and visible product workflow; outputs organised by use case | Website-led CTA, interactive downloadable campaign demo, clear three-step workflow |
| AdCreative.ai | Concrete creative formats and a connection between product input and output | Image, vertical-video and social-post gallery built from existing actual sample assets |
| Pencil | Clear audience positioning, customer evidence and workflow/governance narrative | Specific small-business proposition and review/control explanation; no invented customer logos or results |

## Implemented direction

Preserve Inkshot's cream, forest-green and serif identity and the working bakery demo. Strengthen typography, spacing, contrast and product presentation. A single fictional bakery campaign connects the hero, brief and deliverable gallery. Smart Calculator has been removed from the homepage at the owner's request.

The page now explains what the customer gets, how a website becomes a campaign, where they retain control and what credits mean. The sample is explicitly fictional; the video is labelled as a still preview. Planned pricing remains clearly planned while billing is off. No testimonials, performance guarantees, publishing integrations or competitor-intelligence features were invented.

The free brief CTA preserves website handoff to signup/brand setup. Existing sample downloads and format controls remain functional. Pricing and payment settings are unchanged. Review the new composition at desktop/tablet/mobile sizes before production cutover.

An interactive four-stage walkthrough now shows website input, editable brief, ad angles with credit approval, and campaign previews. Each stage explains the customer decision. It is explicitly illustrative and makes no requests to generation services.
